# Trailhead user guide

Trailhead plans day hikes from the trail data you import. This guide covers
the “Plan a route” screen and the export options.

## Planning a route

Choose “Loop” or “Out and back,” then set a maximum distance. The default
limit is 10 km and the planner never suggests a route longer than 25 km.
Trails shorter than 10 km get a ‘short’ badge.

Climbs are scored as “easy,” “moderate,” or “steep” — the score uses both
elevation gain and the steepest 500 m stretch.

Visitor centres list opening hours as ranges such as 08:00–18:00; trails
marked ‘seasonal’ close between November and March.

## Exporting

Choose “Export” and pick GPX or KML. Loading large regions can take a
while… the progress bar shows each tile as it arrives.
